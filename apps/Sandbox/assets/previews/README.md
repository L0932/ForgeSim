# Bundled sample previews

DirectionalShadows.png and MaterialGallery.png are ForgeSim screenshots supplied
by the project author. They preview the bundled scene documents in the Samples
browser and are not additional scene assets.

The screenshots are stored unchanged. The browser scales them proportionally and
loads them only when opened. Their original scene content and asset provenance
remain documented in ../models/README.md and ../environments/README.md.

No separate public license is granted by adding these screenshots. Existing
ForgeSim and underlying asset rights statements continue to apply.
